## Decision rules

- If it needs a fake number, a fake doctor, or a fear of missing a milestone, it is out.
- If it asks for an account before the parent has seen their month, it is out.
- If it puts a child’s vaccine record into a share sheet, it is out.
- If it is a new surface and the birth-date activation rate is still unknown, it waits.
